CERAMICS / BANKO

Yokkaichi and surrounding areas, Mie

Banko: From Cooking to the Table

Imagine the vessel you cook in becoming the center of the table. Banko offers a route into that connection between preparation and serving, whether you are sharing a large meal or making something warm for one.

2026.11.08 / KOTO

This regional example is separate from the contemporary maker discussed below.

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Begin with your own senses

Similar-looking cooking vessels may support different heat sources. Check direct flame, oven, microwave and induction separately. Then imagine lifting the piece with mitts, washing it in your sink and finding space for it on a shelf. Practical fit is part of the design.

02 / MAKERS TODAY

4TH-MARKET

Yokkaichi-based 4TH-MARKET is a maker-led brand. Its official introduction encompasses tableware, ovenware and direct-heat cooking tools. Instead of beginning with a decorative style, begin with something you would like to cook and serve.

03 / AT YOUR TABLE

Three ideas for your table

The following are KOTO editorial suggestions.

01

A warm meal for one

Use cookware approved for the recipe's heat source and a suitable trivet at the table.

02

Weekend sharing

Put the main vessel in the center and use plain individual plates around it.

03

Soup and a side

Add a small cold side dish to a warm meal, giving one favorite cooking vessel regular use.

Before you choose

Choose for your actual heat source and portion size. Follow the individual instructions on empty heating and temperature changes. The regional name alone does not establish whether a piece is suitable for cooking.

04 / EXPLORE THE REGION

Connect with the region

The federation behind Banko no Sato brings together manufacturing and wholesale cooperatives. It is a route into the region beyond a single brand. Consult the venue's current information for exhibitions, activities and reservation requirements before arranging a visit.

萬古陶磁器振興協同組合連合会・ばんこの里会館 / Banko no Sato ↗
Regional background and sources

Banko ware is known for donabe cooking pots and purple-brown clay teapots. It connects ceramics with cooking and brewing as well as serving. Check each product's instructions for compatibility with direct heat or induction.
