CERAMICS / ARITA

Arita and surrounding areas, Saga

Arita: Give Food a Little Breathing Room

Where you place food on a white plate can change a whole setting. Explore Arita as a tool for everyday meals as well as an object for special occasions.

2026.11.08 / KOTO

This regional example is separate from the contemporary maker discussed below.

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Begin with your own senses

Simple shapes draw attention to rims and depth. Consider whether you want generous empty space or somewhere to hold a sauce. Let appearance and intended use inform each other.

02 / MAKERS TODAY

1616 / arita japan

The official collection includes TY Standard by Teruhiro Yanagihara and Colour Porcelain by Scholten & Baijings. Comparing their forms and colors is one way to explore how designers approach the same production region.

03 / AT YOUR TABLE

Three ideas for your table

The following are KOTO editorial suggestions.

01

Pasta lunch

Leave space around the food; choose enough depth for the sauce.

02

One accent

Add a pale-colored side plate to white dishes you already own.

03

Dinner with friends

Repeat a shape and vary glassware or cloth, letting the food supply color.

Before you choose

The pictured vessel is historical, not a 1616 / arita japan product. Compare current pieces through the official photographs, considering usable surface as well as overall diameter.

04 / EXPLORE THE REGION

Connect with the region

The cooperative’s MONO・NO・SU offers a gallery and shop. Combine a close look at one brand with a broader comparison of makers, checking visitor information before travelling.

佐賀県陶磁器工業協同組合 / Saga ceramic industry cooperative ↗
Regional background and sources

Arita porcelain offers blue-and-white decoration as well as colorful overglaze painting on a white body. Some pieces leave generous open space; others build up patterns. Look at how decoration works with the vessel's shape.
