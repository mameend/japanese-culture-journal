Kyoto and surrounding areas

Kyoto: Kiyomizu Ware for Everyday Living

Kyoto ceramics may suggest something precious kept in a display cabinet. Yet makers also begin with ordinary meals and practical use. The interesting question is how a special object can become part of your everyday table.

2026.11.08 / KOTO

This regional example is separate from the contemporary maker discussed below.

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Begin with your own senses

Rather than defining Kyoto ware through one clay or color, follow individual forms: a bowl to lift, a plate that frames food, a vessel for a single stem. Beginning with use lets you find a maker without first becoming an expert in decorative techniques.

02 / MAKERS TODAY

TOKINOHA / 清水大介・清水友恵

Daisuke and Tomoe Kiyomizu began TOKINOHA in 2009. Its official introduction covers tableware, vases and custom work for professional chefs. Thinking about the meal and vessel together, rather than as separate choices, offers useful ideas for a home table too.

03 / AT YOUR TABLE

Three ideas for your table

The following are KOTO editorial suggestions.

01

A small first course

Serve one vegetable preparation in a modest bowl, leaving its shape visible.

02

Pasta night

Pair a simple main plate with one expressive small bowl instead of making every piece a focal point.

03

A single flower

Place a low vase near the edge, leaving the center free for conversation and serving.

Before you choose

Begin with a plate or bowl you could use several times a week. Consider weight, rim thickness and stacking as well as appearance. The eighteenth-century bowl pictured here is a historical Kyoto example, not a current TOKINOHA work.

04 / EXPLORE THE REGION

Connect with the region

The Kyoto cooperative federation also profiles TOKINOHA. Regional directories help place an individual maker within a wider community. Check the official store information when planning a visit: a central Kyoto shop and a production studio may be in different places.
