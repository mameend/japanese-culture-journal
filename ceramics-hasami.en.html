CERAMICS / HASAMI

Hasami, Nagasaki

Hasami: Everyday Ware for a Small Cupboard

Love tableware but have limited storage? Instead of searching for a plate that does everything, think of three meals you often eat. Let the scale of your daily life guide your introduction to Hasami.

2026.11.08 / KOTO

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Begin with your own senses

Rim angles and depth change capacity even when diameters match. Think about stacked height, ease of retrieval and the dishes you already own. Imagine the object inside your cupboard as well as on your table.

02 / MAKERS TODAY

白山陶器 / Hakusan Porcelain

Hakusan Porcelain’s official shop includes Hira Chawan and Asa no Ito. Compare forms with holding and serving in mind, alongside patterns. Even if you like an entire series, one frequently used piece can be a useful place to begin.

04 / EXPLORE THE REGION

Connect with the region

The town’s kiln map helps locate workshops and shops. Use it to understand distances, then confirm each destination’s current opening days. It can help you plan an ordinary visit as well as an event-day trip.

波佐見町・窯元散策マップ / Hasami town kiln map ↗
Regional background and sources

Hasami has long supplied everyday tableware. Alongside white porcelain and blue-and-white decoration, it offers designs for contemporary tables. Consider how a piece stacks and feels in the hand as you picture using it each day.
