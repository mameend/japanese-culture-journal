## Opening sketch

When we sit down with a bowl of tea, what are we tasting? Alongside aroma and warmth, there is the weight in the palm, the rim at the lips, and the character of the glaze. These, too, become part of the moment. This essay will explore Japanese aesthetics and everyday life through the relationship between tea and the vessel that holds it.
