CERAMICS / TOBE

Tobe, Ehime

Tobe: Blue Patterns for Daily Company

Rice, noodles and a prepared side dish ask a lot of everyday tableware. A piece you reach for repeatedly should be enjoyable to look at as well as useful. Tobe's white and blue can enter your home through one frequently used dish.

2026.11.08 / KOTO

This regional example is separate from the contemporary maker discussed below.

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Begin with your own senses

Compare the energy of a line with the space around it, inside and outside the vessel. A substantial appearance does not tell you the exact weight or thickness. For bowls you lift every day, let both your eyes and your hands inform the choice.

02 / MAKERS TODAY

陶房遊・松田啓司／松田奈織子 / Toubouyuu

In the cooperative's profile of Toubouyuu, Keiji and Naoriko Matsuda describe using their own everyday meals as inspiration. This connection between making and use offers an approachable way to consider a vessel. Explore the balance of form and pattern through the official photographs.

03 / AT YOUR TABLE

Three ideas for your table

The following are KOTO editorial suggestions.

01

Noodle lunch

Pair a patterned bowl with a plain side dish and keep the setting compact.

02

Prepared sides

Two small plates with different blue patterns can work together when their sizes are similar.

03

Breakfast bread

Combine a blue-and-white plate with clear glassware rather than reserving the pattern for Japanese meals.

Before you choose

For pieces you lift often, inspect the weight and rim. A sturdy appearance does not establish dishwasher or microwave compatibility. The historical covered jar pictured here is separate from Toubouyuu's work.

04 / EXPLORE THE REGION

Connect with the region

Follow the cooperative directory to other studios to discover differences within the blue-and-white palette. When seeing pieces in person, consider how they will stack with what you own. Verify each workshop's opening and visiting arrangements separately.

砥部焼協同組合 / Tobe pottery cooperative ↗
Regional background and sources

Substantial white porcelain with blue decoration offers a recognizable introduction to Tobe ware. Imagine it on your everyday table, and notice the weight, the shape of the rim and the rhythm of the pattern.
